## Featured project

### [Pocket — Personal Finance & Expense Tracker](https://github.com/bibasshrestha980-npl/expense_tracker)

A Flutter app for tracking income and expenses, designed around the way people manage money in Nepal.

- Record and review transactions, with dashboard summaries and category insights.
- Import details from bank and digital wallet SMS alerts, including eSewa and Khalti.
- Switch between Bikram Sambat and Gregorian calendars, and use the app in English or Nepali.
- Sync account data with Firebase and export reports as PDF or CSV.
